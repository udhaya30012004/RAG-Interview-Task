  METRIC DEFINITIONS

Recall@5     : What fraction of all relevant chunks are in top-5?

NDCG@5       : Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain — rewards relevant chunks at top

Relevance labeling: keyword-based (15%+ keyword overlap = relevant)
Higher is better for all metrics (range: 0.0 to 1.0)
